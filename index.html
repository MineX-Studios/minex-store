07 — Good to know

Before you buy.

01How fast do I get what I bought?
Ranks, crate keys, passes and boosters are delivered to the Minecraft name you enter at checkout, usually within seconds of the payment confirming. If the server is restarting, delivery completes as soon as it's back.
02Do I need to buy every rank below the one I want?
No. Buy the tier you want directly — it includes everything from every rank below it. Upgrading later is also fine; you keep the higher tier.
03What exactly is in a crate?
Every crate page lists its full reward pool with the real percentage chance for each item, before you buy. Nothing is weighted secretly and there is no "filler" tier that isn't shown.
04How do protection blocks and player listings work?
Protection blocks are owned by players, not the store. When you buy one from a listing, the seller receives 70% of the price and the block transfers to you. Place it to claim a full 3D cube around it, trust whoever you want, and retrieve it later with /fo retrieve.
05What happens if I already own a rank?
Contact us on Discord before purchasing a duplicate and we'll sort out an upgrade or a credit. Ranks don't stack, and buying the same one twice won't give you a second helping of perks.
06Can I get a refund?
Digital goods that have already been delivered generally can't be refunded, but if something failed to arrive or arrived wrong, open a ticket in Discord and we'll fix it or refund it.
