Rank questions

The things people ask.

01Do ranks expire?
No. A rank is a one-time purchase that stays on your account permanently. There is no monthly fee, no renewal and no way to lose it by being inactive.
02Do I have to buy every rank below the one I want?
No. Buy the tier you want directly — it includes every perk from every rank below it. The ladder exists so you can see what you're getting, not to force upgrades.
03How does the weekly split work for King and Emperor?
King and Emperor can be split into four weekly payments of a quarter of the total price. The rank activates after the first payment and stays active — the remaining payments are a schedule, not a subscription.
04Can I upgrade from King to Emperor later?
Yes. Open a Discord ticket before buying twice and we'll arrange the difference. Buying the same rank a second time does not grant extra perks.
05Do rank perks apply on Bedrock?
Yes. Ranks are tied to your account across both Java and Bedrock. Chat prefixes, homes, vaults and kits all work on either edition.
06What if a perk doesn't work after I buy?
Rejoin the server once — perks are applied on login. If something is still missing after that, open a Discord ticket with your username and we'll fix it manually.
