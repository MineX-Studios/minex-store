Booster questions

Before you light it up.

01Does a booster help only me or everyone?
Everyone online while it runs. That's the point — the whole server's shard rate doubles, which is why players often pool boosters at peak times.
02Do unused boosters expire?
No. Boosters sit in your inventory indefinitely until you activate them. The only thing that expires is an hour, once it has started running.
03What if the server restarts mid-boost?
The remaining time is preserved and resumes when the server comes back. You don't lose hours to a restart or a crash.
04Can two boosters run at once?
They queue rather than stack to ×4. A second activation waits until the current hour finishes, so nothing overlaps and nothing is wasted.
05What's the AFK zone bonus?
Shard income in the AFK zone also doubles during a global booster, which is why some players park there during boosted hours.
