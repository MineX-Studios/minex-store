How claims work

Place it, trust who you want.

01 Place the block

Set it down where you want the middle of your claim. Everything inside the cube becomes protected immediately.

02 Trust your people

Add players to the trust list so they can build, open containers and use doors. Everyone else is blocked by default.

03 Retrieve it later

Break the block with /fo retrieve and it goes back to your inventory — the claim moves with it. Nothing is consumed.

Claim questions

Before you claim.

01What exactly does "49³" mean?
It's the length of one side of the protected cube, measured in blocks. A Block II claim extends 24 blocks in every direction from the protection block, for 49 blocks across in total.
02Can I move my claim?
Yes. Break the protection block with /fo retrieve and it returns to your inventory. Place it somewhere else and the cube moves with it — the block itself is never used up.
03What happens if I overlap someone else's claim?
The server refuses to place a block that would overlap an existing claim. You'll get an error and the block stays in your inventory, so nothing is wasted.
04Why is the store price different from /protectionshop?
The store sells player listings, so prices float with supply — sometimes cheaper than the in-game shop, sometimes not. Both routes give you the same block.
05Do I keep the block if I stop playing?
Yes. Claims persist indefinitely and are tied to your account. If you want to hand a base to a friend, add them to the trust list or transfer the block by breaking and trading it.
06How does selling work?
List a protection block you own from the in-game market menu. When it sells, you receive 70% of the price and the block transfers to the buyer.
