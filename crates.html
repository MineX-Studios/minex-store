How it works

Buy a key, open it at spawn.

01 Buy the key

The key is delivered to your player automatically once payment verifies. No ticket, no waiting for staff.

02 Open it at spawn

Walk to the crate of the matching tier at spawn and right-click with the key in hand. The roll happens server-side.

03 Keep or trade it

Most gear is tradeable. Skinned and named items carry their own history, which you can check on the item explorer.

Odds are published, not estimated. Every percentage on this page is read from the same configuration the server rolls against. If a pool changes, these numbers change with it.

Crate questions

Before you open one.

01Are the odds really published?
Yes. Every crate page lists the full pool with a percentage per item. These are the actual roll weights from the server configuration, not marketing estimates.
02Do I need the key in my inventory to open?
The key is delivered to your player. If your inventory is full it stays in the delivery queue and is handed over as soon as you have space — it is never lost.
03Are crate rewards tradeable?
Most gear drops are fully tradeable, and skinned variants keep their own ownership history. A few cosmetics are bound to the account that opened them, and those are marked on the reward list.
04What happens to duplicate rewards?
Duplicates are yours to keep, trade, or salvage for shards. Nothing is silently converted or discarded.
05Can I buy keys as a gift?
Enter the recipient's Minecraft name at checkout instead of your own. Delivery goes to whoever is named there, so double-check the spelling.
